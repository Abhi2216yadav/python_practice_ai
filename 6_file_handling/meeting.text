Today, I’m pleased to present our project **CampusX**, a smart digital platform designed to make the learning experience more organized, interactive, and accessible for students. The main idea behind CampusX is to bring important academic resources such as notes, study materials, assignments, previous-year questions, quizzes, mock tests, and announcements into a single platform. Students can not only access learning materials but also practice through timed tests and track their performance through analytics and rankings. At the same time, teachers can manage academic content and assignments, while administrators can manage the overall platform and users. We have developed CampusX using modern technologies such as **React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Firebase**. Through this project, our aim is to create a centralized digital learning environment that helps students learn better, practice consistently, and monitor their academic progress.
